Artistic Milliners • AM‑4 Washing

Washing WIP Report

About this tool: A live Work‑in‑Process (WIP) tracker for the AM‑4 washing plant. Every gate‑pass is logged once — goods Received from Stitching, later Issued to Finishing, any Returns back to Stitching, Finishing Returns back to Washing, and transfers with other washing units — and the app handles the rest of the math: Received − Returned = Total Received, Issued − Finishing Return = Total Issued, and Total Issued − Total Received = WIP. These roll up instantly into Brand‑wise and Unit‑wise reports, Daily and Monthly views, PO/Style lookups, and live charts — no spreadsheets, no manual formulas.
